About

My full name is Abdul Rahim Mohd Sabri. I studied petroleum engineering at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS. After I graduated, I started an apparel printing business instead.

That business became QR Global Group, founded in 2015. It started as an apparel service provider. Today, through its subsidiaries, it manufactures and sells fabric and textiles.

As Group Managing Director of Kayser Holdings, I run several companies at once. I give each one its own days in my week, and I grow people who can run them on their own.

How I think about business

01

Buy for advantage, not variety

An acquisition should secure supply, lock in distribution or control margin. It should deepen what you already do well.

02

Build leaders, not dependency

A group runs on the people inside it. My job is to grow CEOs and GMs who own their companies.

03

Do the highest-value work

Know which work is worth the most, protect your time for it, and build systems for the rest.

"What is the highest paid and most valuable work that you do?" The question I keep coming back to
